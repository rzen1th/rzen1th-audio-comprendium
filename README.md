A completely free audio library!

You do not have to ask me permission to do whatever you want with these sounds. The contents of this library are completely free for you to download, use, and modify for any NON-PROFIT or NON-COMMERCIAL project (unless I give you express permission); just be sure to credit rzen1th. That's lowercase r, zen, 1 (one), th. 

If you need help, ideas, or want to get in contact with me for any reason, the best way to do so is through Discord. Feel free to DM me @ rzen1th!
